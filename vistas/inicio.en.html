SCHEDULING

Before and after
the consultation.

Symphomed doesn't start when the patient walks in or end when they leave: booking and the next follow-up also take care of themselves, over WhatsApp.

✆

Patients book themselves

They message the practice on WhatsApp, see the slots that are actually free and pick one. The appointment lands in the calendar without anyone answering the phone.

✓

Confirmed instantly

They get the confirmation right away. If they need to cancel, they write “cancel” in the same chat and the slot opens up again.

↻

Follow-up runs itself

When the consultation closes, Symphomed proposes the next follow-up date and sends the patient a link to pick their time.
